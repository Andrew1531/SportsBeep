# 🏀 SPORTSBEEP: The Pulse of the Game, On-Chain

**SPORTSBEEP** is a Cardano-native NFT project that captures the energy of live sports moments and transforms them into collectible, utility-driven digital assets. Built for fans, collectors, and crypto-native communities, SPORTSBEEP delivers real-time engagement, transparent royalty logic, and scalable tokenomics—all on the Cardano blockchain.

---

## 🔗 Project Highlights

- **Blockchain:** Cardano (ADA)
- **NFT Standard:** CIP-68 compliant metadata
- **Royalty Logic:** On-chain, automated, and creator-enforced
- **Utility:** Access to gated content, community rewards, and future staking mechanics
- **Payment Integration:** ADA-native wallet support (Nami, Eternl, Lace)

---

## 🧠 How SPORTSBEEP Works

1. **Mint Moments:** Users mint unique SPORTSBEEP NFTs tied to sports highlights, game stats, or fan-driven events.
2. **Royalty Logic:** Every secondary sale triggers a smart contract that enforces creator royalties—ensuring fair compensation and long-term sustainability.
3. **Utility Unlocks:** NFT holders gain access to exclusive content, voting rights on future drops, and eligibility for community rewards.
4. **Community Growth:** SPORTSBEEP is designed to scale with fan engagement—bringing sports culture and blockchain together.

---

## 💰 Royalty Logic Breakdown

| Event              | Triggered Action                          | Royalty % | Destination         |
|-------------------|-------------------------------------------|-----------|---------------------|
| Secondary Sale     | Smart contract enforces royalty split     | 5%        | Creator wallet      |
| Marketplace Listing| Metadata flags royalty enforcement        | —         | CIP-68 compliant    |
| Wallet Transfer    | No royalty triggered                      | 0%        | —                   |

> SPORTSBEEP uses Cardano’s native royalty enforcement via metadata and smart contracts. No centralized marketplace dependency.
