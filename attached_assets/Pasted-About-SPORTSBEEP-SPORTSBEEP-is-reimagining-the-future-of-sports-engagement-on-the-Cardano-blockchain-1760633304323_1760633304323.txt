About SPORTSBEEP
SPORTSBEEP is reimagining the future of sports engagement on the Cardano blockchain. What began as a fantasy sports concept has evolved into a Web3-powered ecosystem where fans don’t just watch the game—they participate in it.

At the heart of the platform is $BEEP, a fungible token (FT) minted on Cardano. $BEEP fuels access, rewards, and community features inside the SPORTSBEEP app, while also powering liquidity pools and transparent tokenomics designed to build long-term trust.

SPORTSBEEP is more than collectibles—it’s about utility and ownership. Fans can use $BEEP to unlock premium features, vote on platform decisions, and engage in a transparent, trust-driven economy that grows alongside the community.

By leveraging Cardano’s secure, energy-efficient blockchain, SPORTSBEEP delivers a fast, fair, and future-proof experience. No warehouses, no shipping, no middlemen—just a digital-first sports platform built for scale, transparency, and fan empowerment.

⚙️ Tech Stack
Blockchain Layer: Cardano mainnet, with Blockfrost API integration for wallet and transaction services

Token Infrastructure: Native Cardano fungible token ($BEEP), liquidity pools on Minswap, Cardano Token Registry integration

Frontend: React + Tailwind for a modern, responsive UI

Backend: Ruby on Rails + Puma server, with APIs for timers, user authentication, and token utilities

Mobile: Native Android app (in development) for on-the-go fan engagement

Deployment & DevOps: GitHub (repo + SSH), Replit (rapid iteration), cPanel hosting for production builds

Wallet Support: Eternl, Nami, and Lace wallets for seamless user onboarding

Security & Compliance: Transparent policy IDs, registry verification, and long-term tokenomics planning

🌍 Our Mission
SPORTSBEEP’s mission is simple: to create a trust-driven, transparent, and rewarding experience for sports fans worldwide. By blending cutting-edge crypto mechanics with the passion of sports culture, SPORTSBEEP is building a platform that’s fast, fair, and future-proof—empowering fans to connect, compete, and shape the future of sports entertainment.
